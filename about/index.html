About

Built by a fly fisherman, for fly fishermen.

FishFly is a saltwater fly fishing resource — part comprehensive library, part personalized planner.

The Fly Library is a comprehensive catalog of saltwater fly patterns, organized by the destinations that fish them — 204 patterns across 42 destinations today, from the Bahamas to the Bismarck, the Lowcountry to the Nubian Flats. Every pattern carries a verified photo or the Compass silhouette in its place; nothing is fabricated, nothing borrowed from the wrong fly.

FishFly.ai, coming next, will generate a personalized trip brief for the destination and dates you choose: species, weather windows, fly selection, gear, regulations, access — assembled the way a guide would brief you the night before, without the scramble of forty browser tabs.

The conviction behind both: the best saltwater fly resource shouldn't be scattered across out-of-print books, half-remembered forum threads, and a Google Doc you can't find. It should be one place, organized like a fisherman thinks.

Who's behind it

A proper bio lands here once the launch dust settles. For now: one obsessive saltwater fly fisherman with too many destinations on the list and too many flies in the box, building the resource he kept wishing existed.
