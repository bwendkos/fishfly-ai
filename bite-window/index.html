What's in the report

Five signals, one timeline.

Daily rating

A 1–5★ score per day based on how strongly conditions converge. Best, Good, or Weak at a glance.

Hour-by-hour timeline

Stacked rows for light, tide, solunar windows, and wind. See exactly when signals align.

Top 1–3 time blocks per day with reasoning — "first-light flood meets solunar major".

Real data, not estimates
